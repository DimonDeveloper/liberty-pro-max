# iPhone gameplay evidence

User-supplied screenshots and gameplay recording for:
- https://github.com/Awis13/liberty-pro-max/pull/29
- https://github.com/Awis13/x86recomp/pull/9

Captured on an A20 Pro iPhone running iOS 27.2, November 9, 2026.
The screenshots are unmodified copies of IMG_7578.jpeg, IMG_7579.jpeg,
and IMG_7580.jpeg. The Metal HUD shows 2868×1320, Game Mode On, and
individual frame-rate readings of 63.30, 64.57, and 71.29 FPS.
These samples do not establish sustained or stutter-free performance.

The gameplay video is hosted as a release asset in this fork rather than
in either code PR. Its source is the user-supplied GTAIV A20 Pro.mov
(176.7575 seconds, H.264/AAC, 2868×1320). The sharing copy retains the
whole recording and audio, resized to 1920×884 at 60 fps, H.264/AAC.
QuickTime capture changes presentation from Direct to Composited, so
recorded timing is not directly comparable with uncaptured gameplay.

Video: https://github.com/DimonDeveloper/liberty-pro-max/releases/tag/iphone-gameplay-2026-11-09
